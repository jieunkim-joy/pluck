pluck

Pick your favorite photos, together.

How it works

  1. Make a folder. Add up to 100 photos and send the code to your friends.
  2. Swipe. Right to like, left to pass, up for maybe. Look-alike shots come as one card.
  3. See the results. When everyone has finished, see the group's favorites, save them as a PDF or share an image.

Folders and their photos are deleted automatically when they expire.

Get the app

Pluck is in a small test with friends on Android. It will come to Google Play later.
