Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Pluck is an app for choosing your favorite photos together with friends. This page explains what Pluck stores, why, and how to delete it.

What Pluck stores

  • Your Google account basics: your name, email address and profile picture, received when you sign in with Google. Pluck uses them to sign you in and to show who is in a folder.
  • Your nickname and colors: the nickname you choose, and the color you pick in each folder.
  • Photos you add: the photos you upload to a folder, a small preview of each, their size and the time they were taken (used to group similar photos). Photos are saved again before upload, which removes other hidden data such as the GPS location.
  • Your answers: your likes, passes, maybes, ratings, picks between similar photos, and comments in a folder.

Pluck does not use ads, analytics or tracking, and does not read any photos you don't choose to add.

Who can see it

  • Photos, answers and comments in a folder are visible only to the people in that folder. Results show which members liked a photo.
  • People in a folder see your nickname and color.
  • Pluck does not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, which provides Pluck's database, file storage and sign-in. Sign-in uses Google. App updates are delivered by Expo. These services process data only to run the app.

How long it is kept

  • Folders expire after the number of days shown in the app (15 days by default). Then the folder, its photos, answers and comments are deleted.
  • The folder's owner can delete a folder at any time, and you can delete photos you added while photos can still be added.
  • Delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete account. This deletes your account and nickname, the folders you made (for everyone in them), and the photos, answers and comments you added to friends' folders.

Phone permissions

  • Photos: only the photos you pick in the photo picker are read.
  • Notifications: reminders a few days before a folder is deleted. They are scheduled on your phone; you can turn them off in your phone's settings.

Children

Pluck is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
